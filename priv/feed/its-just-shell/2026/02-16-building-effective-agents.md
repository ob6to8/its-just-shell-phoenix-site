Anthropic's own engineering team arrived at the same conclusion as the thesis of this site: the most effective AI systems aren't sprawling autonomous frameworks — they're simple, composable tool loops. Workflows beat agents until they don't, and even then, the winning agents are just well-structured workflows with a model in the driver's seat.
